Caveats that belong in the pitch: resolution is coarse (about 0.5 x 0.625 degrees for meteorology, about 1 degree for solar), wind is at 10 m, there is no globe temperature (radiant temperature uses the uncited solar fallback, TR_FALLBACK_MODE), the hour-stamp convention (start or end of hour) is not yet verified, and the crew is still simulated, so no real-world lead-time or false-alarm claim follows from using real weather.
