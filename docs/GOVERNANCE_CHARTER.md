# HeatBank worker charter (DRAFT v0.1)

**Status: draft written for a competition prototype. It is not legal advice. Before any real deployment it must be reviewed by
a lawyer, an occupational health professional and worker representatives, and translated by a human into the workers'
languages (Thai first).** Nothing in HeatBank has been used with real workers.

## 1. What HeatBank is for
HeatBank helps a crew plan work and rest in hot weather so that people are protected before they are in trouble. Worker protection
comes before productivity. It recommends; people decide. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose anything.

## 2. What it uses
* Weather (forecast or a site sensor that measures the environment only).
* The kind of work, clothing or protective equipment, and whether a group is used to the heat. These describe *types of work*,
  not named people.
* In the prototype: **no personal data, no health data, no measurements taken from any person, no location tracking, no accounts.**

## 3. What it will never do
* Use the data or the alerts for discipline, pay, bonuses, hiring, firing, promotion, ranking, scoring or any decision unrelated
  to protecting health and safety.
* Show a supervisor an individual's health or body data.
* Sell, share or reuse data for advertising or any other purpose.
* Estimate a person's core temperature from a wearable, or monitor individuals.
* Be used to ask people to work longer than the plan allows. The planner can hold or reduce exposure; it cannot extend it.

## 4. What supervisors see
Groups, levels and recommended actions: for example "Zone A: Orange, first break at minute 10, rotate heavy tasks". Groups smaller
than five are pooled so nobody can be singled out. The site status always shows the highest level in any group, so a small group
can never be hidden when it is at risk. No names. No readings.

## 5. If someone may be ill
Anyone can stop work and call the medic or emergency number without needing permission and without penalty. The Red screen offers one tap to call the site medic and one tap to message a buddy whose number the worker keeps on their own phone; the worker starts both and no readings are sent. HeatBank does **not** notify anyone automatically: that would need a channel that identifies the phone, which this design avoids on purpose.

## 6. Choice and control (for any future version that adds personal signals)
* Every personal signal is opt-in, one signal at a time, and can be switched off at any moment without penalty.
* Data stays on the person's own device unless they choose otherwise.
* Nothing is kept longer than the work session unless the worker agrees in writing.

## 7. Honesty about limits
HeatBank uses a standard model for an average worker. It does not know about individual illness, medication, sleep, hydration or
fitness. A Green level is not a guarantee of safety. Thresholds are proposed values. When data are old or missing, the level goes
up, never down, and the screen says so.

## 8. Accountability
* A named person at the employer is responsible for this charter.
* Workers can ask what is collected and how alerts are decided, in their own language.
* Workers can report a problem or a wrong alert to the named person and to their representative without penalty.
* The charter is reviewed after any incident and at least once a year.
