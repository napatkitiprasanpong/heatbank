HeatBank

Planning prototype. All crews, weather and results are simulated. Not a medical device.

A heat budget for every shift.

HeatBank works out how many safe minutes each task allows, hour by hour, with the ISO 7933 heat-strain model. Workers get one level and one action. Supervisors get group levels, never individuals.

Try it in 60 seconds

  1. Open the worker view, choose a profile and drag the hour slider.
  2. Read the "Why this level?" card, then set the connection to stale. The level only goes up.
  3. Open the supervisor view. The 3-person zone is pooled for privacy.

Four rules

Protection first
The plan can hold or cut exposure, never extend it.
Fail safe
Stale, missing or out-of-range data can only raise the alert.
Private by design
No accounts, no identifiers, no uploads. Groups under 5 are pooled.
Humans in charge
Advice plus one-tap help. People decide.
