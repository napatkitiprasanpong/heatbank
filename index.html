HeatBank

Planning prototype. All crews, weather and results here are simulated. Not a medical device.

A heat budget for every shift.

HeatBank works out how many safe minutes of work each task allows, hour by hour, using the ISO 7933 heat-strain model. It turns that into one level and one action for workers, and a group view for supervisors.

GreenNormal work
YellowDrink, break
OrangeReduce, cool
RedStop, cool, buddy
Worker viewWhat one person sees on a phone: level, action, work/rest minutes, heat budget. Supervisor viewCrew levels by zone, first break, rotation. Group figures only, never individuals.

Try it in 60 seconds

  1. Open the worker view and pick a worker type. Drag the hour slider and watch the level climb.
  2. Switch the data condition to stale. The level can only go up, never down, and a banner says why.
  3. Open the supervisor view. The 3-person zone is pooled so no small group is exposed.

Built around four rules

  • Protection before productivity: the plan can hold or reduce exposure, never extend it.
  • Fail safe: stale, missing or out-of-range data can only raise the alert.
  • Privacy by design: no accounts, no identifiers, no upload channel. Groups under 5 are pooled.
  • Humans in charge: advice and a one-tap way to call for help. Workers and supervisors decide.

Works offline once loaded. No tracking, no external scripts, no fonts or data fetched from other sites. Thai text is a draft awaiting native review. Thresholds are proposed design parameters, not clinical guidance.
